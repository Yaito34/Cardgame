### Topic · Sunken Keep
Fantasy: Undead wardens in a **ruined courtyard keep**. Stone and timber **rot and fall apart bit by bit** — the same magic that binds the skeletons **steals time from the surroundings**. Environment pressure is decay and collapse, not built traps and not flood.
Topics: Skeletons · Treacherous dungeon environment (three Environment bases below).
Highlights: Danger (undead), Blocking, Covering, On Reveal / While Close Exhaust, Materials, Scout, Prepare, Spirit.
Scarce: Rations, easy Recovery, forage payouts.
Rules: Repeatable hazard types · one player alone on their track · **natural decay traps only** (no man-made traps in this zone).
**Not in the Zone Deck:** stairs, gates, and named rooms — those are Travel Book Areas or Discovery Book **transitions**. Paths may enhance environment cards (grit on the walk, jammed doors in the ranges) but **roaming Skeletons stay the same** across a floor.
Flavor link: more undead presence = more collapse pressure in fiction (no extra rules keyword).

### Topic · Skeletons
Base Actions (shared names; powers and printed outcomes vary by variant but stay in theme):
[F] Fight — hard pay · Outcomes: Materials, Find Discovery, Spirit (🪬). Disrupts the threat cleanly.
[T] Outmaneuver — cheaper clear · **each variant taxes differently** (not always Exhaust): next-card Covering, next Skeleton +Power, Mage Slip Past imprint, Prepare Traverse, or (Archer) hard empty dodge.
[A] Release Contract — bargaining · unique stretch payoff (Prepare Fight/Work, Scout, Recovery, Find Discovery). Keep effect identities simple; fiction is the Contract itself.
Defaults: Danger on all skeletons except Bone Pile.
Keywords by role: Guard Blocking · Archer On Move Exhaust · Mage wakes Bone Piles (+ Slip Past imprint) · Knight raises all Skeletons · Spearman Reach vs Small Tools.
Power roles: Knight strongest overall · Mage strongest [A] · Guard strong [F] · Archer strong [T] · Spearman average · Bone Pile weakest (very low [T]).

**Mastery lessons:** don’t always Fight; Mage Slip Past is dangerous if Bone Piles exist; Contracts own scarce Recovery/Scout; Spearman punishes Small kits.

### Topic · Environment
Bases under one topic (shared Action families; Power and outcomes vary by card). Sunken Keep uses Obstacle, Blocked Path, Natural Decay Trap, and **Detour**. **Natural decay traps only** (no man-made traps).
**Easy names:** titles must be easy to picture (Broken Floor, Falling Stone, Jammed Door) — see design guide.

Materials polarity (keep sharp):
- Obstacle / Decay Trap [W] often **spends** Materials (build / stabilize).
- Blocked Path [W] often **gains** Materials (break debris).
Do not blur build-vs-scavenge.

Pressure: Blocking / Covering on obstacles and blocks; On Reveal / While Close Exhaust on decay traps **and** a few obstacles. Prefer **next-action / next-card** taxes over path-long flags (easier tracking). Rotting Arch **[T]** may Exhaust and tax the next Work. **Danger** stays mostly on skeletons unless a collapse truly locks retreat.
[A] payoffs should be **typed** (Prepare Traverse / Prepare Work, Scout, Reveal, Recovery, Find) — not generic Prepare 1 on every card.

#### Base · Obstacle (gap / shaft / failing arch — go over or through)
[W] Build Path / Shore / Bridge — hard pay · often **spend Materials** · clean clear.
[T] Climb / Dash / Leap — clear the gap; often **risky Exhaust** or inverted Traverse when fiction is “hard to cross.”
[A] Find Easy Path — Scout / Reveal / Prepare Traverse.

Cards: Floor Hole · Rotting Arch.

#### Base · Blocked Path (passage closed by rubble, boards, jammed door, decay seal)
[W] Clear / Force Open — often **gain Materials** from breaking debris.
[T] Go Around / Squeeze — empty, soft Exhaust, or Reveal +X if the detour is long.
[A] Find Opening — Scout / Reveal / Find / typed Prepare.

Cards: Rubble Block · Wooden Debris · Jammed Door.

#### Base · Natural Decay Trap (unstable floor/stone/wall — no man-made traps)
[W] Stabilize — **spend Materials** to shore; safer clear.
[T] Move Through — risky: empty or soft Exhaust; While Close / On Reveal Exhaust fits here.
[A] Find Safe Path — Scout / typed Prepare / Recovery on the safer route.

Cards: Broken Floor · Falling Stone · Crumbling Wall.

#### Base · Detour (Side Branch — optional spur; lengthens path)
**Continue Past** prints **Detour** (that overcome does not count toward progress). Entering the spur does not print Detour, so it counts.
[—] Continue Past — Power **0**, free clear (Body; no tool).
[W] / [A] Enter / Search / Force — **Find Discovery T0X** (claim at stretch end, **no roll**).

Cards: Broken Wall · Rubble Alcove · Sealed Niche.

Prefer same-level decay (rubble, wooden debris, jammed doors, broken walls) over ladders, hatches, and stair cards. Stairs are Travel Book / Discovery Book, not zone cards.

# Dungeon Card Design Notes

### Zone identity
- **Topics only:** Skeletons and treacherous dungeon environment (no expedition subplot in this deck).
- **Decay fiction:** Undead-binding magic steals time from the stone — environment is collapse and rot, not built traps or flood. Courtyard keep: jammed doors, falling stone, rotting arches. (Path fiction, not extra zone cards).
- **Easy names:** Environment titles are plain and pictureable (Broken Floor, Falling Stone, Jammed Door, Wooden Debris).
- **Repeatable types:** Broken Floor, Rotting Arch, Rubble Block, etc. — not unique named rooms. Stairs and gates are Travel Book / Discovery Book, not Zone cards.
- **Solo-facing:** Every card must make sense for one player on their personal track.
- **Author notes (never cards):** use `### …` blocks, `<!-- HTML comments -->`, or plain text between a `# … [type]` deck header and the first `##` card. Topic bases above use `###` for this reason.

### Composition
- **17 challenges:** 6 Skeleton · 11 Environment (plus Zone overview card — not dealt).
- Skeletons follow **Topic · Skeletons** above.
  - **Danger** on every skeleton except Bone Pile
  - Keywords: Guard Blocking · Archer On Move Exhaust · Mage wake · Knight aura · Spearman Reach vs Small Tools
  - **Cheap clears diversified:** Guard → next Covering · Archer hard empty dodge · Mage Slip Past imprint · Knight → next Skeleton +1 Power · Spearman Inside the Point → Prepare Traverse · Bone Pile Scatter empty
  - Power roles: Knight overall · Mage [A] · Guard [F] · Archer [T] · Spearman mid · Bone Pile weakest
  - Roaming skeleton **cards** stay the same across the keep; **Areas** may raise Count, strip Danger, or add Challenge Power (see `TravelBook.md`)
- Environment follows **Topic · Environment** above.
  - **Obstacle (2):** Floor Hole, Rotting Arch (Dash taxes next Work) — [W] spends Materials
  - **Blocked Path (3):** Rubble Block (Crawl −1 Rations), Wooden Debris (Blocking), Jammed Door (On Reveal Jamb) — [W] gains Materials
  - **Decay Trap (3):** Broken Floor, Falling Stone (Duck → next Env Covering), Crumbling Wall — [W] spends Materials; On Reveal / While Close Exhaust
  - **Detour (3):** Broken Wall, Rubble Alcove, Sealed Niche — Continue Past prints Detour; Find Discovery T01–T03 counts toward progress
  - Prefer same-level rubble / wooden debris / jammed doors / broken walls over ladders, hatches, and stair cards
- **Discoveries (8 dual-face):** fronts **Discovery T01–T08** ([search]) · reward backs ([discovery]) — Treasure (T05 Lore)
- **Hero cards (keep sharp):** Guard, Knight, Mage Slip Past, Rotting Arch, Jammed Door, Wooden Debris, Falling Stone, Sealed Niche.
- **16 tools** and **16 masteries** unchanged below.

### Balance targets
- Hard cards still keep a cheap low/no-reward Action — cheap often **pushes a short tax** (next card / next action), not path-long flags.
- Immediate surprise costs are Exhaust 1–2.
- Average successful overcomes ~2–3 Exhaustion after tools, Help, and Garb.
- **Tracking:** at most one pending “next …” note (same budget as Prepare).
- **Contracts:** keep simple effect identities (Scout, Recovery, Prepare, Find); fiction is Release Contract.
- See `docs/challenge-design-guide.md` for the full design process.
