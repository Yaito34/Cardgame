# Class progression (Tool Training)

How characters grow. Live rules also live in **`CurrentRulesDesign.md`**. Why identity cannot be a power ladder: **`docs/design-goals.md`**.

**Supersedes** Volk + Role as the gate on tools. Every character may **use** every Tool. **Mastery** needs a **Tool Training**. Carry weight is **Backpack Capacity**. **Strength** is an attribute, not the pack.

There is **no skill tree printed on Tools**. The Tool’s subtitle names the Training. The Training entry (lookup / play aid) lists **requirements** and **attribute gain**. If the Tool face has space, print the Training’s requirements there too so you do not have to look up the Training to know what you need.

---

## Use vs mastery

Anyone may Equip and use any found Tool. Training does not raise Power. It unlocks **Mastery** on Tools that name that Training — extra verbs / mini-games, same Power band.

A Tool is mastered when **both** are true:

1. You have taken that Tool’s Training.
2. You own the card.

You never master a Tool you have not found. Taking a Training does not add ghost masteries for unfound cards in that Training.

Four Equip slots remain the loadout.

---

## EXP (not Spirit)

Exploring grants **EXP**. Spend EXP to take **one** legal Training. Spirit stays the buy / pack / Repair / Heal wallet. Do not spend EXP on supplies.

EXP is **personal** and **repeatable** wherever you play (hop-in). A **max character level** caps how many Trainings you can take. After the cap, further EXP does not buy new Trainings; it may pay **respec** at a settlement. New Tools and recipes stay the reason to explore.

You cannot take a Training until you **own at least one** Tool that names it (starter kit counts).

Hit the cap **early**. Collection after that is horizontal (finds inside Trainings you already have, or finds that argue for a respec).

---

## Attributes

Start at **0** in all attributes. Attributes are **keys** for later Trainings, not Power on Commit.

**Base four**

| Attribute | Typical Trainings |
|---|---|
| **Strength** | Heavy / direct tools |
| **Agility** | Setup, mobility, precise lines |
| **Intelligence** | Craft, devices, prepared solutions |
| **Wisdom** | Spirit, insight, support verbs |

**Mix attributes** exist only when a mixed Training is designed. That Training **requires** parent attributes and **grants +1** to a **new** attribute that represents the mix — not another point in Strength or Wisdom.

Example: a Training requires **Strength 1** and **Wisdom 1**, and grants **+1 Faith**. Later Trainings may require **Faith**.

Do not invent a mix attribute for every pair up front. Name one when you add that Training.

---

## One spine per attribute

For each attribute, **only one Training continues that attribute** at a given step.

There is only one Training that **needs Strength 1 and grants +1 Strength**. Same at 0, 2, …: one `0 → +1 Strength`, one `Strength 1 → +1 Strength`, one `Strength 2 → +1 Strength`.

Several Trainings may **require** Strength 1. The extras must **leave** Strength: they grant a **mix** (Faith) or **another** existing attribute (Agility, Wisdom, …). After Strength 0 you might spend on:

- Spine: `Req Strength 1 → +1 Strength`
- Mix: `Req Strength 1` (and maybe Wisdom 1) `→ +1 Faith`
- Bridge: `Req Strength 1 → +1 Agility` — only when that Training’s Tools really are Strength doing another column’s job

You still must **own a Tool** from that Training. The find lights the fork; the **gain** line is what makes it a different life, not a second Strength pack.

**Mix is the default fork.** A mix attribute does not skip another column’s level 0. **+1 to another base** is a bridge; use it sparingly or the four starting Trainings stop meaning much.

The spend choice is **another Strength (spine)** vs **Agility / Intelligence / Wisdom** vs a **mix** — not two competing Strength-1 bundles. The cap makes that choice real.

---

## Filing new Tools

| The new Tool is… | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| Same grammar as an existing Training’s toys | **Add it** to that Training |
| Different enough | **New Training** with the same Req, but a **different gain** (mix or other attribute) |

Never print a second Training that needs Strength 1 **and** gives Strength. Expansion must not create two Strength spines.

**All Tools in a taken Training are legal to master** (when you own the card). They are more options for the **4 Equip** bar, not a second pick at level-up. Two characters with the same Trainings should still differ by which Tools they found and packed.

Keep a Training to a handful of Tools (about 3–5) so one spend is not a dump.

---

## What a Training prints

Each Training is a named bundle (several Tools + their Masteries). Lookup shows:

- **Requires:** attribute totals (and “own ≥1 Tool from this Training”)
- **Gain:** +1 to one attribute (the spine of this Training, or the mix attribute)
- **EXP cost** (playtest dial; often 1)

**Level 0** (first Training in an archetype): **Requires** nothing (attributes 0). **Gain** +1 to that archetype’s attribute. Example: a Strength Training at level 0 requires **0**, gives **+1 Strength**.

Deeper same-archetype Trainings require a higher value in that attribute (paid by the **one** spine Training at the previous step). Other Trainings that share that Req grant a mix or a different attribute. See **One spine per attribute**.

Taking a Training **unlocks** later Trainings only in the sense that your attributes now meet their numbers. There is no separate tree diagram on the Tool. Finds reveal Trainings; the graph *is* the requirement numbers. Optional ruin-art may show the tree as fog, not a complete player aid.

---

## Tools (no tree on the card)

Print:

- **Subtitle:** Training name (the mastery school)
- **If space:** `Req …` copied from that Training (e.g. `Req —` or `Req Str 1, Wis 1`)

Looking up the Training is only required when you want the **gain** line or the full Tool list. Requirements on the Tool are enough to know “can I take this Training yet?”

Recipe / hidden Tool faces stay fog until claimed. Do not spoil the whole Training’s catalog on the Tool.

---

## Building while playing

- **Visible next:** owned Tools whose Training you have not taken, and whose **Req** you meet (or almost meet).
- **Fog:** Trainings you have never seen a Tool for; mix attributes you have not earned.
- **All-in:** take the unique spine Training (`Req Strength 1 → +1 Strength`) so the next Strength step opens.
- **Mix / bridge:** at the same Req, take a Training that grants Faith or another attribute instead of more Strength.
- **Bar:** Tools inside a taken Training are extra loadout options, not extra EXP spends.

Same EXP progress → same number of Trainings, not the same sheet.

---

## Card / table tracking

Track on the character: current **Strength, Agility, Intelligence, Wisdom**, plus any mix attributes you have at least +1 in; list of **taken Trainings**; EXP; level vs cap.

Do not track mastery per unfound Tool. Mastery is live iff taken Training + owned card.

---

## Design constraints

- Masteries are verbs and timing, not `+Power` on the same action.
- Easy path numbers stay tuned to **unmastered** use.
- EXP is not Spirit. Efficiency may **change EXP gained**; it must not steal the Heal bill.
- Cap size: about enough Trainings for one deep column + one extra, or two shorts + one mix — not every Training in the game.
- Volk / Role use-gates are retired. Playtest Volk Body cards may remain until the catalog is rewritten; they print **Backpack Capacity**, not Strength.
