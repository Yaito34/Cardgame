# Garb slot jobs (everyday zones)

Playtest sketch. **Does not replace** the live hole list in **`docs/garb-doll.md`**. Overview page: **`GarbSystem.html`**.

Zones are **everyday dressing names**. Armour does not get extra holes; it fills these. **Underwear is never a piece** (no shirt-as-slot, no socks-as-slot). Empty Inner means no jacket / no trousers layer, not “naked.”

Garb **Awareness** is visible marks on an **outer** piece (fashion, colours, symbols, runes), not the 🪬 currency. Inner and Middle never print Awareness. A closed **Wrap** that is a coat hides Awareness on Body Outer.

Empty doll holes still print **Stamina** squares (universal). Those boxes are part of the live stamina pool; overflow can **Damage** the empty hole (see **`CurrentRulesDesign.md`**).

---

## Zones

| Zone | Layers | Everyday | Armour fills the same holes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head** | **2** (under + over) | Cap, hat, hood | Coif under helmet; helmet in Over |
| **Neck** | **1** | Scarf | Gorget / mail collar |
| **Coat / tabard** | **1** (under Neck) | Coat, cloak, or tabard | One hole. Name the garments, not “Wrap” — most readers will not guess coats and tabards from Wrap. |
| **Body** | **3** | Stacked cloth (linen, wool, heavy cloth) | Padded jack, mail, plate (chest, shoulders, **and arms**) |
| **Legs** | **3** | Stacked cloth (trousers, over-trousers, waxed) | Padded hose, mail, plate (thighs / knees) |
| **Feet** | **1** | Shoes, boots | Greaves + sabatons **into** the boot |
| **Hands** | **1** | Gloves | Gauntlets |

**Trinkets** (belt, wrist bands, earrings, rings, brooches, and similar) are **item cards like Tools**. They are not doll holes and they print **no** resource slots. Pouches stay Kit. Arm plate stays on Body Outer, not on a wrist card.

Pauldrons, cuirass, rerebrace, couter, vambrace are **one** Body Outer plate, not separate slots.

## Body and legs: three layers, any material

Same rule on both columns. A hole is **closer to the body** or **further out**, not “the mail hole.” Fill with **armour or cloth** for Fight, Work, Traverse, weather.

| Layer | Typical cloth | Typical armour | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inner** | Linen, padded linen | Padded jack / padded hose | Mix Stamina + a little specific. Lowest: simple linen **or** padded linen. |
| **Middle** | Wool, leather | Mail | More specific, less Stamina. |
| **Outer** | Heavy wool | Plate | Most Fight, least Stamina. Coat / tabard is a separate hole. |

**Coat / tabard** is one hole under the neck. A cloak counts as a coat. You do not wear a coat and a tabard at once. A closed coat hides Awareness on Body Outer.

**Legs** use the same three. Heavy Outer/Middle here **tax Traverse** more than the same materials on Upper body. Feet still own walking (boots).

Underwear stays off the doll. Inner linen is a **worn jacket or trousers**, not a chemise.

## Head: use two layers

**Yes — Under and Over.** Not a third head hole.

Medieval kit already stacks here for both fight and weather: padded/mail **coif under a helmet**, and on the road a **cap under a hood or hat**. One hole would force *padded coif and iron helmet* as a single card, or make you take the helm off to wear the hood. Two holes keep the names honest (*padded linen coif*, *iron helmet*, *wool hood*).

That is the same move as a skull cap under a hood: two layers beat one against wind and water. The doll should allow that without turning Head into another 3-stack.

| Layer | What goes here | Do not put here |
|---|---|---|
| **Head Under** | Skull cap, coif, thin cap (against the skull) | Scarf, gorget, cloak |
| **Head Over** | Hood, hat, helmet (the one you see) | A second hat on top of a hood — pick one Over piece |

**Neck** is the third weather piece (scarf), and the one that makes turning your head worse. That matches “cap + hood, and a scarf when it is really cold.” Do not add Head Middle for the scarf.

Everyday you often fill **only Over** (a hat) or **only Under** (a cap). Empty is normal. Both filled is winter or armour.

- Helmet in Over → Fight, costs Awareness.  
- Hood/hat in Over → weather / Awareness.  
- Under cap/coif → Cold / Water help, a little universal; padded coif adds a little Fight.

## Single-layer zones

| Zone | Job | Heavy fill |
|---|---|---|
| **Neck** | Scarf (Stamina / weather) or gorget (Fight). May print Awareness. | Not a cloak |
| **Feet** | Traverse, then Rain resistance | Plate boots = less Traverse, a little Fight |
| **Hands** | Work | Gauntlets = Fight up, Work down. May print Awareness. |

## Trinkets (not on the doll)

Belt, wrist bands, earrings, rings, brooches, and similar are **separate cards**, same status as Tools: you can own them, they are not a hole on the garb sheet, and they never have S / F / W / T / A / C / R squares.

---

## Mix without hit zones

Upper body and legs are two large 3-layer batteries. Same stamina types; kit them apart.

- Both columns heavy: lots of Fight, little universal, worse Traverse from legs.  
- Both light: weather and mixed days, thin Fight.  
- One heavy, one light (or a missing piece): the midpoint.

Head Under+Over is a **small** version of that for weather and helmets, not a third Fight column.

---

## Catalog names

Shape words, not hole codes on the face: *padded linen jacket*, *mail*, *iron plate (chest, shoulders, and arms)*, *wool trousers*, *iron helmet*, *padded linen coif*, *wool hood*, *leather boots*. Inner linen is a jacket or trousers, never “underwear.”
